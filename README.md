# My-health-system-
My health system architecture 
I was create 11 organ tracking system for my future time 

Max sensor 



   1. Brain – The control center of your nervous system.
   2. Heart – Pumps blood through your circulatory system to deliver oxygen.
   3. Lungs – Allow you to breathe by taking in oxygen and removing carbon dioxide.
   4. Liver – Cleans your blood, removes toxins, and produces bile for digestion.
   5. Kidneys – Filter waste and excess water out of your blood to create urine.
   6. Stomach – Uses acid and enzymes to break down the food you eat.
   7. Small Intestine – Absorbs vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients from your food.
   8. Large Intestine – Absorbs remaining water and processes waste into stool.
   9. Pancreas – Makes insulin to control blood sugar and enzymes for digestion.
   10. Spleen – Filters your blood and helps fight off infections.
   11. Skin – The largest organ of your body, protecting everything on the inside.
